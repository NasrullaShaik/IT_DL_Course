Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services—servers, storage, databases, networking, software, analytics, and more—over the Internet ("the cloud") to offer faster innovation, flexible resources, and economies of scale. Instead of owning computing infrastructure or data centers, companies rent access to services from cloud providers.

Key Characteristics
- On-demand self-service – Users can automatically provision computing resources.
- Broad network access – Services are available over the network and accessed via standard mechanisms.
- Resource pooling – Resources are pooled across multiple customers.
- Rapid elasticity – Resources can be quickly scaled up or down.
- Measured service – Usage is monitored, controlled, and reported.

Cloud computing has evolved from traditional on-prem hosting to globally scalable, API-driven, cost-effective platforms. It is now a cornerstone of digital transformation.
